The Premise

Less noise.
More story.

We've all built a stronger muscle for tuning things out. AI-generated slop, relentless content, everyone competing for the same few seconds of attention. So when someone tells a genuine, human story that helps us understand our world — or ourselves — a little better, it's a moment we remember. Sometimes for good.

Whether it's a networking event, the first thirty seconds on a stage, or the line before an investor pitch, you get one opportunity where the people on the other side are actually open to hearing something they haven't heard before. Something revelatory. Something iconic.

Most introductions lead with what you do. The iconic introduction leads with why you do it — and says just enough that people in the room feel like they understand something true about you, and find themselves thinking, if not saying, "tell me more."

How It Works

One journey.
