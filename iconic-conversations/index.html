The Premise

Less noise. More room.

Founders and operators keep telling me the same thing. They're tired. Not of the work. Of the noise around it. They want to reach the people who actually matter to them. They want to deliver something valuable. They want connection that doesn't require posting through a content treadmill.

The options usually feel binary: contribute to the noise, or sit it out.

Iconic Conversations is the third option. A real room, a real conversation, a real working session — built for the people doing the work, not the people performing it.

What Happens

Ninety minutes. Three movements.

01

The Conversation

A single in-depth interview with one guest who is actively making the shift from operator to icon. Not a panel. Not a Q&A. A real conversation, on the record.

02

The Breakouts

Structured small-group rooms where we actually do something with what was just said — apply it, pressure-test it, translate it to your own work.

03

The Room

Twenty-one people, including me. Approved in advance. Camera on. Phones away. The whole point is the people you'd want next to you in a room like this.

Who This Is For

Built for a specific kind of operator.

Seats are approved one by one. The room is small on purpose, and curated so the conversations during breakouts are worth the time. If you see yourself in one of these, apply.

  1. Founders and co-founders

    Building companies past the early operator phase, ready to step into a more recognizable role in their category.

  2. CEOs and senior executives

    Leading companies through transitions — M&A, succession, hypergrowth, or a public-facing identity shift.

  3. Chiefs of staff & executive partners

    Directly supporting a founder or CEO and shaping how they show up in the market.

  4. Marketing & PR professionals

    Whose work centers on founders or senior leaders, and who want a sharper read on how authority actually gets built.
