The moment you're in

You've earned the credibility.
Your opening should make the room lean in.

Your old title, company, book, or category used to validate you. Going forward, the “Next You” requires a new opening.

For years you may have been introduced by a title, a company, a book, or a body of work that carried the room for you.

Now you're leaving corporate life, launching an advisory or fractional platform, refreshing a message, or stepping into the next chapter of your mission — and the opening that used to land no longer creates the same pull.

You don't have a credibility problem — it's a positioning one. Your audience will follow you into the new chapter — unless your introduction still points to the old you.

What this is

A one-day creative working session —
not a workshop, a class, or a conference.

Your New Opening Line is a concentrated day of story, stagecraft, strategic identity work, candid feedback, and filmed rehearsal. You walk in using the introduction of who you were. You walk out with a newly crafted opening that defines who you've become — one you actually love saying. A sharper, more human 60–120 second opening that makes people lean in, remember, and want more.

The day, step by step

Six moves from your old opening
to the one you'll keep.

01

Diagnose

Capture your current introduction on stage and pinpoint exactly why it no longer fits the chapter you're entering.

02

Distill

03

Chart the old story, the new story, and the turning point between them into a clear narrative arc.
