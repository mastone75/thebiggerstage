Live · In Person
02 — The Revelation Intensive

On camera.
Witnessed.
In person.

A one to two-day in-person experience for a small cohort of founders. Each founder goes on camera, one at a time, witnessed by peers in the room. Hearing another founder make the shift often unlocks your own.

This is not a workshop. There are no slides. The camera is the method: it makes the shift real in a way that a conversation in private cannot. What you say in the room becomes an asset. What you witness becomes a mirror.

What each founder leaves with

  • Pre-event intake and intention-setting session with Matt
  • On-camera Revelation session, witnessed by the cohort
  • Footage and professionally edited clips
  • Full transcript of the conversation
  • Narrative analysis and messaging debrief
  • Cohort connections that typically outlast the event
The Format

6 to 10 founders. One to two days, in person. Structured intake and intention-setting in advance. On the day, each founder goes on camera while the group witnesses. The intimate scale is the point.

Co-founders

Co-founder pairs are especially welcome. There is something about going through the process side by side that creates alignment you can't get from a separate session. Many co-founders arrive uncertain and leave unified.

Who It's For

Founders who have been too close to their own story to tell it clearly. Founders ready to go on camera for the first time, or who have tried and gotten it wrong. Founders entering new markets, bigger rooms, and harder conversations.

The Investment

Investment is discussed individually during a Clarity Call. Cohort size keeps it personal. Details, logistics, and next dates are confirmed on the call.

Inquire about the next Intensive

Dates and locations are set cohort by cohort. If you're interested in joining or bringing a group, a Clarity Call is the right place to start. We'll look at where you are, what the Intensive could unlock, and whether the timing is right.
